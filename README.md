## 🧠 How I Build AI Products

My product thinking starts with the **user problem**, not the technology.

I focus on:

- Understanding the user, context, and core problem
- Defining product vision and MVP scope
- Translating problems into requirements and user stories
- Designing user journeys and AI interaction flows
- Identifying where AI should assist vs. where deterministic workflows are better
- Designing Human-in-the-Loop checkpoints
- Defining AI boundaries, fallback behavior, and failure paths
- Considering AI reliability, explainability, and user trust
- Prototyping quickly and validating product ideas
- Iterating based on product goals and user experience

My goal is to make complex AI systems feel **simple, useful, trustworthy, and intuitive**.
